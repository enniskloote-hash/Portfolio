The Challenge

Sky was the first connected product launched on a new AWS platform — giving the team direct visibility into how consumers and dealers actually used the product in the field. Concept development began in 2015 and launched in China in 2017.

The work involved significant cross-org complexity: tying authentication, registration, and order management together across multiple internal systems, while navigating market-specific constraints on what data could be surfaced to users. Sky was also the foundational product for the AWS platform that other connected products would later depend on for shared services like security and data infrastructure.

Process

How We Approached It

We refined the initial concept from key screens to full workflows and vetted each with the development team to confirm backend capabilities matched the frontend design. Work ran in sprints with quarterly planning and staged releases.

A significant design challenge was identifying which product data was actually meaningful to consumers — not just what was technically available, but what would be useful enough to change behavior.
