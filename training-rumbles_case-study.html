The Challenge

Every team knows AI matters. Most are buried in think pieces, vendor pitches, and generic frameworks that never quite match how their organization actually works. What's missing is someone who can look at their specific workflows and show them where AI can actually help — and what to do about it first.

That clarity doesn't come from a slide deck. It has to be built in real time, with the people who live inside the work — surfacing the friction points, the repetitive decisions, the places where a well-placed tool could multiply capacity or cut drag. The output has to be specific enough to act on, which means it has to be built from their work, not borrowed from someone else's.

Process

How We Approached It

The Rumble format is structured enough to guarantee a real artifact at the end, and open enough to go where the work actually is. Every engagement moves through the same three phases — align, map, prioritize — but the content comes entirely from the client's actual workflows, not a predetermined template.
