About Me

Better products start with aligned teams.

I have built multiple teams that have delivered multi-modal product experiences in various markets, including consumer, B2B, medical, and automotive. My background in Industrial Design, which I apply today in UX (HCD), drives me to understand and seek out users' unmet needs, and address them in the execution of the product or service.
